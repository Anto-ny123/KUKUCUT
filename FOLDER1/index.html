Our Story

Bringing Kenyan Families Together Through Quality Food

Our journey began with a simple idea: to provide Kenyan families, restaurants, and businesses with fresh, high-quality chicken they can trust. Growing up in Kenya, we saw how important good food is in bringing people together.

Chicken is at the heart of many of our meals, from family dinners to celebrations and street food favourites. But we also noticed that many customers struggled to find consistently fresh, hygienically handled, and fairly priced chicken products.
